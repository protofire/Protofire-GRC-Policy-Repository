## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how [DAO Name] (“we”, “us”, “our”) and its members, acting as joint controllers, collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data when you interact with our decentralized platform, smart contracts, and associated interfaces.

We are committed to respecting your privacy and complying with applicable data protection laws, including:

EU/EEA: General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

USA: CCPA, CPRA, and other state-level laws

Canada: PIPEDA

LATAM: LGPD (Brazil), and other regional frameworks

Important Notice on Blockchain Data:

Due to the inherent nature of blockchain technology, certain information (such as wallet addresses, transaction metadata, and governance votes) is permanently recorded on-chain. While this data is generally pseudonymous, it may, in some circumstances, be linked to a natural person and therefore constitute personal data. Such data cannot be altered or deleted once written to the blockchain. By interacting with our services, you acknowledge and accept this characteristic of blockchain-based systems. If you do not agree with this type of data processing, you should refrain from using our platform and services.
