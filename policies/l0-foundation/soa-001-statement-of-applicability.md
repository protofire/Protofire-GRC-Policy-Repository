# 1. Purpose

This Statement of Applicability (SoA) declares which ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls apply to Protofire's Information Security Management System (ISMS), the implementation status of each, and the justification for inclusion or exclusion. It is the authoritative reference for internal audit, external certification, and client due-diligence.

# 2. Scope

The SoA applies to the ISMS scope defined in L0-4 (ISMS Scope Statement): all Protofire Web3/DeFi delivery engagements (Track A platform operations and Track B client delivery), corporate services supporting them, and the GRC programme itself. It covers all personnel, contractors, and systems processing Protofire or client information assets.

# 3. How to read this document

Applicable = Yes: control is in scope of the ISMS and Protofire is obligated to implement or justify partial implementation.

Applicable = No: control is excluded; the Justification column explains why (e.g. remote-first organisation has no physical entry points under Protofire control).

Status values: Implemented (evidence available), Partially implemented (in progress or covered by compensating controls), Planned (target in current cycle), To assess (pending CISO review).

Primary artefact points to the Protofire policy, standard, procedure, or register that operationalises the control. Multiple artefacts may apply; only the primary is listed here.
