# CL-409 — DPIA Screening Checklist

## Scope
Screening checklist to determine whether a full DPIA is required for an engagement.

## Checklist

| # | Question | If YES | If NO |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Does the engagement process EU personal data? | Continue | DPIA not required |
| 2 | Is the engagement tier T2 or above? | DPIA mandatory (POL-011-R04) | Continue |
| 3 | Does processing involve automated decision-making? | DPIA mandatory (GDPR Art.35(3)(a)) | Continue |
| 4 | Does processing involve large-scale profiling? | DPIA mandatory (GDPR Art.35(3)(b)) | Continue |
| 5 | Does processing involve systematic monitoring of public areas? | DPIA mandatory (GDPR Art.35(3)(c)) | Continue |
| 6 | Does processing involve sensitive/special category data? | DPIA mandatory | Continue |
| 7 | None of the above triggers met? | DPIA not required. Document screening result. | — |

## Output
- Screening result: DPIA Required / Not Required
- Signed by: CISO (DPO)
- Recorded in: REG-510
